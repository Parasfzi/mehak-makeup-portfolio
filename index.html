About

Mehak Tomar

With over eight years of experience transforming faces for bridal ceremonies, editorial shoots, and fashion weeks across New York, Paris, and Milan, Mehak Tomar has established herself as one of the most sought-after makeup artists in the industry.

Her philosophy is simple: every face tells a story, and makeup is the language. She blends classical technique with avant-garde creativity to craft looks that are simultaneously timeless and modern.

✦ Featured in Vogue, Harper's Bazaar & Elle
✦ Official Artist for NYFW 2022–2025
✦ Certified by Kevyn Aucoin Beauty School
